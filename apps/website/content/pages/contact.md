**If anyone is in danger, call 911 first.**

## Email

Email us at [{{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}](mailto:{{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}). We read every message. lokl doesn't have a phone line.

## About a booking

Open the booking under **My bookings**. From there you can cancel, or tell us the provider didn't show up. For anything else about a booking, email us and include the listing's name and the date.

## If you host on lokl

Report a problem from your dashboard: open **Help**, then **Report a problem**. You'll follow it there, and we'll email you when we reply. Safety reports reach the whole team straight away.
