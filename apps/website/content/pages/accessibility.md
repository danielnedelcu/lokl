We want everyone to be able to find, book and host on lokl, including people who use a screen reader, a keyboard, voice control, a switch, zoom, or other assistive technology.

## The standard we work to

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA, on the website and in the hosts' dashboard.

## What we do

- Everything can be done with a keyboard, with a visible focus outline.
- Buttons and links are big enough to tap.
- Colour is never the only way something is shown: states also have words or icons.
- Images have text descriptions, and form fields have labels and clear error messages.
- If your device asks to reduce motion, smooth scrolling is turned off, and dialogs, menus, panels, the search bar, carousels and photo zoom don't animate.
- We check pages with an accessibility checker (axe) and by keyboard as we build them, and our automated tests include keyboard-only journeys.
- We write in plain language.

## What isn't right yet

- The accessibility checker isn't yet run automatically on every change.
- The "Continue with Google" sign-in button comes from Google, and we haven't yet tested it fully with screen readers.

## Tell us about a barrier

If something on lokl is hard or impossible for you to use, please tell us. Email [{{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}](mailto:{{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}) and say:

- which page, and what you were trying to do;
- what happened;
- the device, browser and any assistive technology you use, if you can.

We'll reply, and fix what we can.
