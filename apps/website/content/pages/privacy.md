lokl is a marketplace where you can find and book Services and Experiences from local people in Atlanta. The people who offer them are called hosts.

## The short version

- We collect what we need to run bookings: who you are, how to reach you, and what you booked.
- We don't sell your personal information.
- Hosts see your contact details only once a booking is accepted, and only for that booking.
- We use a few outside companies to run lokl, such as Stripe for payments. They're listed below.
- You must be 18 or over to use lokl.

## What we collect

**When you sign in.** Your email address. We email you a code or a link to sign in; there's no password. If you choose **Continue with Google**, Google shares your name, email address and profile photo with lokl.

**When you book.** Your name, email address and, if you give it, your phone number. Notes you write for the host. For a Service where the host comes to you, the address where they should come, and your city and zip code. What you booked, when, for how many people, and what you paid.

Please only put in your notes what the host needs for the booking. If you choose to share something about your health or access needs, we use it only for that booking.

**When you pay.** You pay on Stripe's secure payment page. Stripe collects your card details; lokl never sees or stores your full card number.

**When you host.** Your business name, your area, and the profile you write (headline, bio and photos). Your listings, with their prices, photos, and the address where they take place. Your hours, time off and booking settings. To pay you, Stripe collects your identity and bank details directly; lokl doesn't store them.

**Reviews.** Your rating and what you write. Reviews show your first name and last initial (for example, "Sam T.") and the month of the booking.

**Saved listings and hosts.** The listings and hosts you save, so you can find them again.

**Problem reports and messages.** If you're a host, the problems you report to us and our replies. If you email us, your email and what you write.

**How you use the site.** See "How we measure the site", below.

## How we use it

- To run your bookings: to take payment, tell the host what they need, send you emails about your booking, and pay hosts.
- To keep lokl safe and fair: to check reviews against our [review rules](/review-rules), look into reports, and act on misuse.
- To answer you when you contact us.
- To understand how people use lokl, so we can improve it (see below).
- To meet our legal, tax and accounting duties.

## Who sees what

- **Everyone** can see hosts' public profiles and listings, and published reviews.
- **Hosts** see your name, notes and party size when you book or request. For a Service at your place, they see only your city and zip code until they accept. Your email, phone and full address are shared with the host once your booking is accepted, and only for that booking.
- **Customers** see a host's exact address only once their booking is confirmed.
- **lokl's team** can see bookings, messages and reports, to run lokl and help when something goes wrong.

## How we measure the site

We use Google Analytics to understand how people find and use lokl: which public pages are viewed, what people search for (the area, dates and kind of listing, never anything typed), which listings are opened, and how many people start and finish a booking. Google Analytics sets cookies (`_ga` and `_ga_…`) that last about two months, and Google keeps this data for two months.

We don't send Google your name, email address, phone number, address, messages, or anything that identifies a booking. We never measure your account or dashboard pages, or the sign-in pages. Google's advertising features (Google Signals and ads personalisation) are off.

**Turning it off.** If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we don't load Google Analytics at all. You can also install Google's opt-out add-on: https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

## Cookies

- **Signing in:** a cookie keeps you signed in, for up to 8 hours at a time. Another remembers which page to return you to after you sign in.
- **Saving before you sign in:** if you press a heart before signing in, a cookie remembers it for 30 minutes, so it's saved once you do.
- **Google Analytics:** `_ga` and `_ga_…`, about two months, as described above.

lokl doesn't use advertising cookies.

## Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control

If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we don't load Google Analytics at all. Browsers' older "Do Not Track" setting has no agreed meaning, so we don't act on it; Global Privacy Control covers the same choice.
