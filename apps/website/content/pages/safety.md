**If anyone is in danger, call 911 first.**

## What lokl does

- **Experiences are reviewed.** lokl reviews each Experience before it goes live. Services go live when the provider publishes them. lokl can take any listing down.
- **Providers are paid through Stripe.** To be paid, every provider gives Stripe, our payments provider, their identity and bank details, which Stripe checks as payments require.
- **Payments stay on lokl.** You pay on Stripe's secure page, never in cash or by transfer to someone you met on lokl.
- **Addresses stay private until you book.** A provider's exact address is shown only to customers with a confirmed booking. A customer's email, phone and address are shared with the provider only once a booking is accepted, and only for that booking.
- **Reviews come from real bookings.** Only a customer whose booking took place can review it. lokl removes reviews only under the [review rules](/review-rules).
- **Contact details stay out of public text.** Reviews, replies and provider profiles can't contain phone numbers, emails or web addresses.

## What lokl doesn't do

- lokl doesn't run background checks on providers or customers.
- lokl doesn't provide insurance for bookings.
- lokl can't promise a time to respond. We read every report.

## If something goes wrong

- **During a booking:** if you feel unsafe, leave, and call 911 if anyone is in danger.
- **If you host on lokl:** report it from your dashboard (**Help**, then **Report a problem**, then **Safety**). Safety reports reach the whole team straight away.
- **If you're a customer:** email us at [{{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}](mailto:{{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}), with the listing's name and the date. If the provider didn't show up, report it from the booking.
