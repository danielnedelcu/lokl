## Your account

- You must be 18 or over to use lokl.
- You sign in with a code we email you, or with Google. Keep access to your email account safe: anyone who can read your email can sign in as you.
- Give your real name and correct contact details when you book, so the host can reach you.

Our [Privacy Policy](/privacy) explains what we collect and why.

- **Experiences** run on set dates. You choose a date and how many people are coming, up to {{PARTY_SIZE_MAX}}, and pay when you book.
- **Services** are booked at a time from the host's free times. Some book straight away, and you pay when you book. For others, the host accepts first: your card is held, not charged, until they do. They answer within {{ANSWER_WITHIN}}, and at least {{ANSWER_BEFORE_START}} before the start. If they don't, or they decline, the hold is released and you're not charged.

- You can cancel for free until {{CANCEL_FREE_BEFORE}} before the start. If you book less than {{CANCEL_FREE_BEFORE}} before, you can cancel for free for {{CANCEL_GRACE}} after booking (or until the start, if that's sooner).

## If something goes wrong

- If the host didn't show up, tell us from your booking, from the start until {{NO_SHOW_REPORT_UNTIL}}. We look into it, and we hold the host's payment while we do.
- For anything else, email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}.

## Reviews

- You can review a booking that took place, for {{REVIEW_WINDOW}} after it.
- Reviews must follow our [review rules](/review-rules). We remove a review only if it breaks them, never for being negative.
- Write about your own experience, honestly. Don't accept anything in return for a review, and don't offer anything for one.

- arrange to pay a host outside lokl for a booking made on lokl;
- copy, scrape or interfere with lokl.

We may pause or close an account that breaks these terms.
