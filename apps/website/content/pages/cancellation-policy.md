title: Cancellation policy
summary: When a booking can be cancelled, and what's refunded. Refunds are always in full.

---
Refunds are always in full: lokl doesn't keep a fee when it refunds you.

## When a customer cancels

| When | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| A request the provider hasn't accepted yet | The hold on the card is released. Nothing is charged. |
| {{CANCEL_FREE_BEFORE}} or more before the start | Full refund. |
| Within {{CANCEL_GRACE}} of booking, before the start | Full refund. |
| Less than {{CANCEL_FREE_BEFORE}} before the start, otherwise | No refund. The provider is paid. |
| After the start | It can't be cancelled. |

## When a provider cancels

A provider can cancel a booking before it starts, and gives a reason the customer sees. The customer always gets a full refund. If a provider cancels an Experience session, everyone booked on it gets a full refund.

## When lokl cancels

The customer always gets a full refund, and the provider isn't paid for that booking.

## If the provider doesn't show up

The customer can report it from the booking, from the start until {{NO_SHOW_REPORT_UNTIL}}. lokl looks at every report, and holds the provider's payout meanwhile. If we agree, the customer gets a full refund.

## If a customer doesn't show up

There's no refund, and the provider is paid.

## Changing a time

A booking can't be moved to another time. Cancel it, if the rules above allow, and book again.
