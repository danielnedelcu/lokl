## lokl's commission

It's free to list on lokl. When you're booked, lokl takes a commission from the price:

| Kind of listing | lokl's commission |
| --- | --- |
| Services | {{COMMISSION_SERVICE}} |
| Experiences | {{COMMISSION_EXPERIENCE}} |

You receive the rest. Each booking shows what you'll receive.

Customers pay the price you set: lokl doesn't add a fee on their side. Payment processing is paid by lokl from its commission.

If lokl changes a rate, bookings already made keep the rate they were made with.

## When you're paid

1. The booking takes place.
2. {{PAYOUT_AFTER_END}} after it ends, lokl sends your share to your Stripe account.
3. Stripe pays it into your bank on its own schedule.

A payout waits while lokl looks into something: a customer reports you didn't show up, a customer disputes the payment with their bank, the booking was refunded, your account is paused, or your Stripe account can't receive money yet.

## Cancellations and refunds

- If a customer cancels less than {{CANCEL_FREE_BEFORE}} before the start, you're still paid, unless they cancel within {{CANCEL_GRACE}} of booking.
- If you cancel, or lokl cancels, the customer gets a full refund, and you aren't paid for that booking.

See the [cancellation policy](/cancellation-policy) for every case.
