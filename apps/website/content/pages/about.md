## Who we are

lokl is a marketplace for local Services and Experiences. On lokl, you can book someone to cut your hair or fix your bike, or join a food walk or a workshop. The people you book are local. We started in Atlanta, and Atlanta is where lokl works today.

## Who we serve

### Customers

You can search by where, when and what, and see each listing's price, place and reviews before you book. You book and pay on lokl, and you pay the price shown. You can cancel for free until {{CANCEL_FREE_BEFORE}} before the start.

### Hosts

Hosts are the local people and small businesses who offer Services and Experiences. It's free to list. You set your prices and your hours, and lokl takes a commission only when you're booked. Your share is sent to your bank through Stripe, our payments provider, after each booking.

### Atlanta

Every listing on lokl is in Atlanta today.

## What we stand for

### Clear fees, published

Customers pay the price shown, and lokl doesn't add a booking fee. Hosts can see exactly what lokl keeps: {{COMMISSION_SERVICE}} of a Service and {{COMMISSION_EXPERIENCE}} of an Experience. See [Fees and payouts](/fees).

### Honest reviews

Only people whose booking took place can leave a review. A review is never removed for being negative: only for breaking the [review rules](/review-rules).

### Your privacy

We don't sell your personal information. Addresses stay private until you book: a host's exact address is shown only to customers with a confirmed booking, and a customer's address is shared with the host only once a booking is accepted. See [Safety on lokl](/safety).

### Accessibility

We want everyone to be able to find, book and host on lokl, whatever device or assistive technology they use. See [Accessibility](/accessibility) for the standard we work to, and what isn't right yet.

## How it works

1. **Find something.** Search or browse Services and Experiences in Atlanta.
2. **Book and pay.** Choose a time or a date, and pay on lokl's secure payment page.
3. **Go, then review.** Your booking is under **My bookings**. Afterwards, you can leave a review.

See [How lokl works](/how-lokl-works) for each step in full.
