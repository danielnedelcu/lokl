lokl is where you find and book local people: someone to cut your hair, fix a bike or teach a class, or an Experience to share, like a food walk or a workshop.

We started in Atlanta, and Atlanta is where lokl works today.

## What you can book

- **Services**, booked at a time that suits you, from the provider's free times.
- **Experiences**, on set dates, with a set number of spots.

## How it works

You book and pay on lokl, and the provider is paid through lokl after your booking. See [How lokl works](/how-lokl-works).

## Hosting on lokl

If you have a skill or an Experience to share, you can list it. See [How hosting works](/hosting).
