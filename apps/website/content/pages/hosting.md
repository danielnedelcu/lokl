## Who can host

Anyone 18 or over with a Service or an Experience to offer in the Atlanta area can set up a business on lokl. It's free to list: lokl takes a commission only when you're booked. See [Fees and payouts](/fees).

## Getting started

1. **Set up your business profile:** your business name, area, and a short profile customers see.
2. **Connect Stripe to get paid.** Stripe, our payments provider, sends your earnings to your bank. It takes about 5–10 minutes.
3. **Create your first listing.**

## Services and Experiences

- **A Service** is booked at a time that suits the customer, from your free hours. You choose whether customers book instantly, or you approve each booking. A Service goes live when you publish it, once your payouts are set up.
- **An Experience** runs on dates you set, for a group. lokl reviews each Experience before it goes live, and may send it back with a note.

## Your time

You set your weekly hours, hours for particular dates, time off, how much notice you need, and time between bookings. You can't be booked twice at once.

## Getting paid

Your share is sent {{PAYOUT_AFTER_END}} after each booking ends, then reaches your bank on Stripe's schedule.
