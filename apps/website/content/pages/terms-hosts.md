## Who can host

- You must be 18 or over, and offer your Services or Experiences in the Atlanta area.
- You provide the Service or Experience yourself, or through your business, and you're responsible for doing it well and safely.
- lokl doesn't provide licences, permits or insurance for your work: getting any your work needs is up to you.
- Give true details about yourself, your business and your listings.

## Getting paid

- lokl pays you through Stripe, our payments provider. To get paid, you set up a Stripe account from your dashboard and give Stripe your identity and bank details. Stripe's own terms for connected accounts apply to that account.
- Customers pay lokl, and lokl keeps a commission: {{COMMISSION_SERVICE}} of each Service booking and {{COMMISSION_EXPERIENCE}} of each Experience booking. The rate is the one shown on [Fees and payouts](/fees) when the booking is made; a later change doesn't affect it. Listing is free.
- Your share is sent {{PAYOUT_AFTER_END}} after each booking ends, then reaches your bank on Stripe's schedule.
- We hold a payment while we look into something: a customer's report that you didn't show up, a card dispute, or a problem with your Stripe account. We release it, or refund the customer, once it's settled.
- You're responsible for your own taxes.

## Your listings

- Describe each listing truthfully: what's included, how long it takes, where it happens, and the price.
- Use only photos you have the right to use.
- Don't put phone numbers, email addresses or web addresses in your profile, listings, or replies to reviews.
- Experiences are checked by lokl before they go live, and may be sent back with a note. Services go live when you publish them, once your payouts are set up.
- We may take down a listing that breaks these terms, and we'll tell you why.

## Your availability and bookings

- Keep your hours, time off and booking settings up to date: customers book from them.
- If you approve each booking, answer each request within {{ANSWER_WITHIN}}, and at least {{ANSWER_BEFORE_START}} before the start. If you don't, the request expires and the customer isn't charged.
- Turn up on time, as booked.
- If you must cancel, cancel from your dashboard as early as you can, and give the customer a reason. The customer gets a full refund, and you're not paid for that booking.

## Customers' details

- Once you accept a booking, you see the customer's email, phone and address for it. Use them only for that booking.
- Your access to them ends 30 days after the booking (LAUNCH-PLACEHOLDER: true once that limit is built). You keep the customer's name and the booking itself.
- Don't add customers to mailing lists, or share their details with anyone else.

## Reviews

- Customers can review a booking that took place. You can reply once to each review, following our [review rules](/review-rules).
- Don't offer anything in return for a review, and don't ask customers to change or remove one.
- We remove a review only if it breaks the rules, never for being negative.

## Pausing or closing an account

We may pause your account if you break these terms or put customers at risk. While it's paused, your listings are hidden and payments due to you are held while we look into it. We'll email you to tell you. You can close your account by emailing {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}.
