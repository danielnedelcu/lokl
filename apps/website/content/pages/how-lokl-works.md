## 1. Find something

Search by where, when and what, or browse Experiences and Services in Atlanta. Each listing shows its price, where it happens, and its reviews.

## 2. Book it

- **An Experience** runs on set dates. Choose a date and how many people are coming, up to {{PARTY_SIZE_MAX}}.
- **A Service** is booked at a time that suits you, from the provider's free times. Some book straight away. For others, the provider accepts first: your card is held, not charged, until they do. They answer within {{ANSWER_WITHIN}}, and at least {{ANSWER_BEFORE_START}} before the start.

You need an account to book. There's no password: sign in with a code we email you.

## 3. Pay

You pay the price shown, on Stripe's secure payment page. lokl doesn't add a booking fee.

## 4. On the day

Your booking is under **My bookings**, with the time and where to go. For a Service at your place, the provider gets your address once the booking is confirmed.

## 5. Afterwards

You can review your booking for {{REVIEW_WINDOW}}. Reviews come only from people whose booking took place.

## If plans change

See the [cancellation policy](/cancellation-policy). If the provider or lokl cancels, you always get a full refund.

More step-by-step help is in the [Help centre](/help/customers).
