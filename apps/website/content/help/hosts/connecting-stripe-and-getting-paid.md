lokl pays you through Stripe, a payments company. Stripe checks your identity and keeps your bank details: lokl never sees them.

## Set up payouts

1. In your dashboard, open **Payouts**.
2. Choose **Set up payouts**. You'll go to Stripe.
3. Follow Stripe's steps. It takes about 5–10 minutes. You'll need your bank details and your Social Security number or business tax ID.
4. When you're done, Stripe sends you back to lokl.

If Stripe needs more details, the page says **Finish setting up payouts**. Choose **Continue setup** to pick up where you left off.

## When you're set up

The page says **Payouts are set up**. Choose **View payouts in Stripe** to see your payouts or change your bank account.

Services can't go live, and Experiences can't be approved, until payouts are set up. See [When payouts arrive](/help/when-payouts-arrive).
