Customers can review a booking for {{REVIEW_WINDOW}} after it ends. You see your reviews under **Reviews** in your dashboard.

## Reply to a review

1. Open **Reviews**, then the review you'd like to answer.
2. On the booking, choose **Reply**.
3. Write your reply: up to {{REPLY_MAX}} characters. Don't include phone numbers, emails or web addresses.
4. Choose **Post reply**.

Your reply shows under the review, from your business name. You can choose **Edit reply** or **Delete reply** at any time. Each review gets one reply.

## Reviews you think break the rules

Reviews are never removed for being negative. If one breaks the [review rules](/review-rules), choose **Report** under it on your listing's page. lokl decides under those rules.

## Ratings

Your rating shows once you have {{RATING_MIN_REVIEWS}} reviews. **Top rated** means an average of {{TOP_RATED_AVERAGE}} or more from at least {{TOP_RATED_REVIEWS}} reviews.
