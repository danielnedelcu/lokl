If anyone is in danger, call 911 first.

## Report a problem

1. In your dashboard, open **Help**, then choose **Report a problem**.
2. Choose what it's about: Bookings, Payments and payouts, My listing, The website, Safety, or Something else.
3. Say what happened, when, and what you'd like us to do.
4. If it's about a listing or a booking, choose it.
5. Choose **Send report**.

A Safety report goes to the whole team straight away.

## Follow your report

Your reports are under **Help**, each with its status:

- **Received:** we've got it.
- **We're looking into it.**
- **Waiting for your reply:** we asked you something. The **Help** menu item shows how many are waiting.
- **Resolved.**

We email you when we reply. Reply on the report's page.

If the problem is sorted, choose **This is sorted**. Only lokl closes a Safety report. If a problem comes back within {{SUPPORT_REOPEN}} of being resolved, reply on the same report to reopen it. After that, choose **Start a new report about this**.
