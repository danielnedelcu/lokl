lokl has two kinds of listing:

- **A Service** is booked at a time that suits the customer, from your free hours: a haircut, a lesson, a repair.
- **An Experience** runs on dates you set, for a group: a tour, a class, a tasting.

## Create a listing

1. In your dashboard, open **My services** or **My experiences**.
2. Choose **Create a Service** or **Create an Experience**.
3. Fill in the title, description, category, price and how long it takes.
4. Add at least one photo, with a short description of what it shows.
5. Under **Location**, say where it happens: your place (with its address), or, for a Service, at the customer's place (with the areas you travel to). Customers see the exact address only once they've booked.
6. For a Service, choose **How customers book**: **Customers book instantly**, or **I approve each booking**.
7. Choose **Save draft** to finish later.

## Going live

- **A Service** goes live when you choose **Publish your Service**. Your payouts must be set up first.
- **An Experience** goes to lokl for review when you choose **Submit for review**. lokl may send it back with a note saying what to change. It can't be approved until your payouts are set up.

lokl can take a listing down if it breaks the rules, and always tells you why.
