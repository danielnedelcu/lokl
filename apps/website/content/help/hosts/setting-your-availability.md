Customers book a Service from your free times. Open **Availability** in your dashboard. Your hours apply to all your Services.

## Weekly hours

Under **Weekly hours**, set the times you work on each day of the week.

## Hours for specific dates

Under **Hours for specific dates**, set different hours for a particular day, such as a holiday.

## Time off

Under **Time off**, block days or times when you can't take bookings. Only you see the note you add.

| **Time between bookings** | None, up to {{BUFFER_MAX}} | None |

**Time between bookings** keeps time free after each booking, for travel or tidying up.

## What customers see

**What customers will see** shows your next free times, as customers will.

## No double bookings

A time booked for one of your Services is taken for all of them, so you can't be booked twice at once.
