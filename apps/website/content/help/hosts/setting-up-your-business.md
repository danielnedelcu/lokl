To list Services or Experiences on lokl, you set up a business. Your dashboard shows three steps:

1. **Set up your business profile**
2. **Connect Stripe to get paid**
3. **Create your first listing**

## Set up your business profile

1. Choose **Start hosting**, and sign in if you need to. See [Signing in](/help/signing-in).
2. In your dashboard, open **Business profile**.
3. Enter your **Business or host name** and choose your **Metro area**.
4. Add a **Headline** and **About you** if you like. Customers see them on your public profile. Don't include phone numbers, emails or web addresses: profiles with them can't be saved.
5. Add a profile photo and a cover photo if you like. We remove location details from photos when you upload them.
6. Choose **Save changes**. Choose **See your public profile** to see what customers see.
