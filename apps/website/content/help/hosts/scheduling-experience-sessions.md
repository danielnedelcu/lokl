An Experience is booked on the sessions you add. Each session has a date, a start time and a number of spots.

## Add a session

1. Open the Experience from **My experiences**.
2. Under **Sessions**, choose **Add a session**.
3. Choose the **Date**, the **Start time** and the number of **Spots**.
4. To repeat it, choose **Repeat every week**, and say **How many weeks in total?**: {{SESSION_SERIES_WEEKS}}, including the first.
5. Choose **Add session** (or **Add sessions**).

## Change or cancel a session

- A session with no bookings can be changed.
- A session with bookings can only be cancelled. Everyone booked on it gets a full refund, and we email them.
