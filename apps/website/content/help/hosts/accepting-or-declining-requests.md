If a Service is set to **I approve each booking**, customers send a request. Their card is held, not charged, until you answer.

## Answer a request

1. Open **Bookings** in your dashboard. New requests are under **Requests**, with the time left to answer.
2. Choose **Answer this request**.
3. Choose **Accept** or **Decline**.

You have {{ANSWER_WITHIN}} to answer, and you must answer at least {{ANSWER_BEFORE_START}} before the start, whichever comes first. If you don't answer in time, the request ends and the customer isn't charged.

## What you see

- Before you accept: the name the customer gave, their notes, and, if you go to them, their city and zip code.
- Once you accept: their email, phone number and full address. Use them only for this booking.

The time is kept for the customer until you answer.

## Cancelling a booking

You can cancel a booking before it starts. Choose **Cancel this booking** and say why: the customer sees your reason. The customer gets a full refund, and you won't be paid for it.
