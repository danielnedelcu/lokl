A Service is booked at a time that suits you, from the provider's free times. Each Service books in one of two ways, and the listing tells you which.

- **Instant:** you pay when you book, and you're booked straight away.
- **Approve:** the provider accepts each booking first. Your card is held, not charged, until they do.

## Book a Service

1. Open the Service you'd like to book.
2. If you're not signed in, choose **Sign in to book**. See [Signing in](/help/signing-in).
3. Choose **Check availability**. Pick a day, then a time. Times are in Atlanta time.
4. Choose **Continue**.
5. Enter **Your name**. You can add a phone number and notes for the provider.
6. If the provider comes to you, enter the address where they should come.
7. Choose **Book and pay** (Instant) or **Request to book** (Approve). You'll go to Stripe's secure page.

## If the provider approves each booking

- Your card is held, not charged, until the provider accepts.
- They answer within {{ANSWER_WITHIN}}, and at least {{ANSWER_BEFORE_START}} before the start, whichever comes first. The exact time is shown before you pay.
- If they accept, you're charged and booked, and we email you.
- If they decline, or don't answer in time, the hold is released and you aren't charged.
- Until they accept, they see your name, notes, and, if they come to you, only your city and zip code. Your email, phone and full address are shared once they accept.
