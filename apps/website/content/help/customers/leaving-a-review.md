Reviews come only from customers whose booking took place. Each booking can be reviewed once.

## Write a review

1. Open **My bookings** from the account menu, and choose the booking.
2. Under the review section, choose **Your rating**: 1 to 5 stars.
3. Write what it was like, and what others should know before booking: {{REVIEW_MIN}} to {{REVIEW_MAX}} characters.
4. Choose **Post review**.

Your review shows straight away, with your first name and last initial. Don't include phone numbers, emails or web addresses: reviews with them can't be posted.

## Edit or delete it

You can edit or delete your review for {{REVIEW_WINDOW}} after the booking ends. After that, it stays as it is.

## What happens next

The provider can post one public reply. A listing shows its average rating once it has {{RATING_MIN_REVIEWS}} reviews. Read the [review rules](/review-rules) to see when lokl removes a review. Reviews are never removed for being negative.
