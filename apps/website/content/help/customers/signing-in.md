lokl doesn't use passwords. Signing in for the first time creates your account.

## Sign in with a code

1. Choose **Sign in**.
2. Enter your email address and choose **Send me a code**.
3. We email you a 6-digit code. Type it into the boxes, then choose **Sign in**.

The code works for {{SIGN_IN_CODE_LASTS}}. If it runs out, send a new one.

The same email has a sign-in link. The link only works in the browser where you asked for it. If you open it somewhere else, type the code instead.

## Sign in with Google

If you see **Continue with Google**, you can use your Google account instead. Google shares your name, email address and profile photo with lokl. If your Google email is already on lokl, it signs you in to the same account.

## No email?

- Check your spam or junk folder.
- Make sure the address is spelled right. Choose **Use a different email** to change it.
