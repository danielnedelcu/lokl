An Experience runs on set dates, with a set number of spots. You pay when you book, and you're booked straight away.

## Book an Experience

1. Open the Experience you'd like to book.
2. If you're not signed in, choose **Sign in to book**. See [Signing in](/help/signing-in).
3. Under **Choose a date**, pick one of the dates shown. Times are in Atlanta time.
4. Choose **How many people?**: up to {{PARTY_SIZE_MAX}}, and no more than the spots left.
5. Enter **Your name**. You can add a phone number and notes for the host if you like.
6. Choose **Continue to payment**. You'll go to Stripe's secure page to pay.
7. Pay on Stripe's page. When you come back, your booking shows as confirmed, and we email you.

## While you pay

Your spots are kept for you for {{CHECKOUT_HOLD}} while you're on the payment page. If you leave without paying, they're freed again.

## After you book

- Your booking is under **My bookings** in the account menu.
- The host sees your name and any notes. They see your email and phone number once you're booked.
- If you need to cancel, see [Cancellations and refunds](/help/cancellations-and-refunds).
