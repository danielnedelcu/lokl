## Save a listing or a provider

1. Choose the heart on a listing or a provider's page.
2. If you're not signed in, sign in first. We save it once you're back.

## Find what you saved

Open the account menu and choose **Saved**. Your saved list is private: providers can't see who saved them.

You can save up to {{SAVED_MAX}} listings and {{SAVED_MAX}} providers. Choose the heart again to remove one.
