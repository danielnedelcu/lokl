Refunds are always in full: lokl doesn't keep a fee when it refunds you.

## When you cancel

| When you cancel | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| A request the provider hasn't accepted yet | The hold on your card is released. You aren't charged. |
| {{CANCEL_FREE_BEFORE}} or more before the start | Full refund. |
| Within {{CANCEL_GRACE}} of booking, before the start | Full refund, even if it's less than {{CANCEL_FREE_BEFORE}} before the start. |
| Less than {{CANCEL_FREE_BEFORE}} before the start, otherwise | No refund. The provider is still paid. |
| After the start | You can't cancel. |

## Cancel a booking

1. Open **My bookings** from the account menu, and choose the booking.
2. Choose **Cancel booking** (or **Cancel request**).
3. The box shows what you'll get back. Confirm to cancel.

## If the provider or lokl cancels

You always get a full refund, and we email you. A provider who cancels gives a reason, which you'll see on your booking.

## If the provider didn't show up

1. Open the booking under **My bookings**.
2. Choose **The provider didn't show up**, and tell us what happened.
3. You can report it from the start until {{NO_SHOW_REPORT_UNTIL}}.

We look at every report. While we do, the provider isn't paid for the booking. If we agree, you get a full refund.

## Changing a time

You can't move a booking to another time. Cancel it (if the rules above allow) and book again.
